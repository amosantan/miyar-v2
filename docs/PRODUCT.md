# MIYAR Product Definition

## Product Identity

MIYAR—مِعيار, “standard” or “benchmark”—is a design-decision intelligence platform for UAE property development. It translates project intent into measurable design, cost, market, risk, sustainability, and investment evidence before major capital is committed.

MIYAR is not a replacement for licensed designers, engineers, quantity surveyors, valuers, compliance consultants, or legal and financial advisers. It is an evidence-oriented decision-support system that improves the speed, consistency, and defensibility of early decisions.

## Primary Users

### Real-estate developer or investment team

Needs rapid feasibility, budget alignment, market differentiation, risk visibility, and board-ready decisions across one project or a portfolio.

### Development and design manager

Needs a structured brief, typology-aware space programme, material direction, fit-out boundaries, cost guardrails, compliance awareness, and traceability.

### Interior designer or consultant

Needs an actionable starting brief, room-level requirements, material allocations, visual direction, finish schedules, and RFQ-ready information.

### Executive, board, lender, or partner

Needs a concise, explainable result showing assumptions, evidence, risks, scenario trade-offs, and reproducibility.

### Platform administrator and intelligence operator

Needs governed benchmarks, sources, ingestion health, audit trails, model/logic versions, override control, and data-quality monitoring.

## Core User Journey

1. Capture project intent through structured fields, text, files, images, voice, URLs, or drawings.
2. Review and approve extracted or suggested project inputs.
3. Generate or edit the typology-aware space programme and fit-out scope.
4. Evaluate strategic, financial, market, design, and execution dimensions.
5. Compare scenarios, risks, sensitivities, and predictive ranges.
6. Generate material quantities, allocations, specifications, cost ranges, and sustainability evidence.
7. Review provenance, freshness, assumptions, confidence, and market comparisons.
8. Produce investor, design, material-board, RFQ, PDF, DOCX, and shareable outputs.
9. Capture actual project outcomes and improve governed benchmarks without uncontrolled self-modification.

## Product Capabilities

### Decision intelligence

- Five-dimension MIYAR score: strategic alignment, financial feasibility, market positioning, differentiation/design suitability, and execution risk.
- Explainability traces, penalties, risk flags, logic versions, and benchmark versions.
- Scenario comparison, sensitivity, stress testing, bias detection, and recommendation ranking.

### Market intelligence

- UAE source registry, evidence vault, provenance, freshness, and reliability grading.
- Dubai Land Department analytics, area intelligence, transaction and rental signals.
- Benchmark governance, proposals, snapshots, competitor intelligence, and trend detection.

### Design intelligence

- AI-assisted design briefs and recommendations.
- Space programming, amenity taxonomy, fit-out/shell-and-core classification, and drawing extraction.
- Material library, quantity intelligence, allocation editing, finish schedules, palettes, RFQs, and visual generation.

### Economics, risk, and sustainability

- Cost ranges, forecasts, Monte Carlo simulation, ROI/value-add models, and portfolio analysis.
- Risk surfaces, stress tests, customer health, alerts, sustainability snapshots, and digital-twin calculations.

### Outputs and collaboration

- Investor summaries, design briefs, material boards, reports, PDF/DOCX generation, comments, audit logs, and expiry-aware public share links.

## Non-Negotiable Product Principles

### Evidence before assertion

Every authoritative number must resolve to a deterministic method, governed benchmark, evidence record, or clearly labelled assumption. Confidence and freshness must be visible when they affect decisions.

### UAE context first

Use AED, local units and terminology, UAE market evidence, local authority context, and local procurement realities. Global evidence must be labelled and adjusted explicitly.

### AI assists; governed logic decides

LLMs may interpret unstructured inputs, suggest materials, write narratives, and create visual direction. They may not set authoritative scores, costs, quantities, thresholds, grades, or compliance decisions.

### Human ownership of material decisions

Users approve key inputs, overrides, benchmark promotions, scoring/financial policy changes, and irreversible actions. AI output must never silently replace explicit user data.

### Explainability and reproducibility

Material outputs preserve input, logic, benchmark, evidence, and generation identity sufficient for review and reproduction.

### Board-ready by default

Outputs prioritize clarity, professional formatting, visible assumptions, risks, disclaimers, and decision relevance.

### Tenant safety and controlled sharing

Organization data stays isolated. Public sharing exposes only explicitly intended read-only content through scoped, expiring tokens.

## Product Boundaries

MIYAR must not:

- Present concept estimates as tendered or contractually guaranteed prices.
- Claim legal, regulatory, engineering, valuation, investment, or compliance certification.
- Invent sources, supplier quotes, transaction evidence, or confidence.
- Automatically promote scraped evidence into authoritative benchmarks without governance.
- Execute purchases, contracts, communications, or production changes without authorization.
- Hide insufficient data behind precise-looking numbers.
- Use customer data to cross-contaminate organizations.

## Success Measures

Product success should be measured by outcomes rather than feature count:

- Time from initial project input to a reviewable decision package.
- Percentage of material numbers with traceable provenance.
- Benchmark coverage and freshness by location, typology, tier, and category.
- Prediction calibration against actual outcomes.
- Reduction in manual briefing, feasibility, and scenario-analysis time.
- Report acceptance and share engagement.
- Frequency and severity of tenant, data-quality, or calculation incidents.
- User override patterns that reveal weak assumptions or missing evidence.

## Decision Authority

| Decision | Authority |
|---|---|
| Narrative, extraction, design suggestion | AI-assisted with human review |
| Calculation, score, quantity, range | Deterministic versioned code |
| Benchmark/evidence promotion | Governed human/admin workflow |
| Project input and override | Authorized project user |
| Compliance interpretation | Qualified professional using MIYAR evidence |
| Production release or migration | Authorized engineering/operations owner |
