## Required source-policy decisions

The official pages state copyright, terms, and download constraints. A platform reviewer must record, for each exact artifact:

1. `termsApproved` — direct retrieval is permitted under the authority's terms.
2. `retentionApproved` — MIYAR may retain the raw artifact or only metadata/hash/OCR-derived locators.
3. `licensingApproved` — the intended internal and tenant/public citation use is permitted.
4. Exact artifact URL, retrieval timestamp, HTTP metadata, byte SHA-256, parser version, and permitted storage reference.

Until those decisions are recorded, the regulatory fetcher remains fail-closed and no source version can become authoritative.
